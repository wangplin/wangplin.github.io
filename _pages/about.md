### M.S. student @ MST

*Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU)*

Peilin Wang is currently an M.S. student at the [School of Microelectronics Science and Technology](https://mst.sysu.edu.cn/), Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU), supervised by [Prof. Mingyu Wang](https://mst.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/wangmingyu). His research interests include **chiplet-based architectures, on-chip/package networks, and multi-chiplet GPU architectures**.

He received his B.Eng. degree in Microelectronics Science and Engineering from the School of Microelectronics Science and Technology, Sun Yat-sen University.
